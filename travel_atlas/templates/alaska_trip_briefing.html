Booking checklist

  1. Flights first — search Kayak or Google Flights. Pick a daytime Sep 30 departure from FAI (no red-eyes). Reasonable 1–3 hour connections via SEA, PDX, or ANC.
  2. Hotel — book Clarion (or chosen property) with a verified 2 queen room name before paying. API bed verification may be incomplete — confirm on Booking.com.
  3. Airport transport — plan taxi/rideshare or hotel shuttle (no rental car assumed). Budget ~$80–120 round-trip for 2.
  4. Optional: rental car — only if you want Chena self-drive or DIY aurora from Chena Rd pullouts (see Optional add-ons).
  5. Optional: Chena Hot Springs — Mom's priority if she wants the soak; book day pass (with car) or day shuttle (without car).
  6. Optional: guided aurora tour — recommended without a car, especially if clouds look likely (~2 weeks out).
  7. Farmers market — plan Saturday Sep 26 morning if landing before ~2 pm.
  8. Pack warm layers; aurora viewing is often late evening.
